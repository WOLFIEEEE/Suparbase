# Code of conduct

Suparbase is a small, focused project, and the only rule we really
care about is the obvious one:

> **Be kind. Don't be a jerk.**

The longer form, in case anyone needs the specifics:

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Disagree about code, not about people. The author of a PR is not
  the PR.
- Default to "yes, and" over "no, but" when reviewing proposals from
  newcomers, they're more useful even when wrong than they look.
- Credit the people whose work you build on, even when it's small.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, or sustained sniping.
- Harassment in issues, PRs, discussions, or any other Suparbase space.
- Doxxing, sharing private contact details, or publishing security
  reports before a fix has shipped.
- Sexual or violent imagery in technical content. Keep it work-safe.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers can:

- Edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that violate
  this code.
- Temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour makes
  the project unwelcoming.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, open a private
report through GitHub's security advisory form
(<https://github.com/WOLFIEEEE/Suparbase/security/advisories/new>) or
contact the maintainers directly. All reports are handled with
discretion.

## Scope

This code applies in every Suparbase space: issues, PRs, discussions,
the hosted instance's support channels, and any official Suparbase
event online or offline.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant][covenant]
v2.1 and trimmed to suit a small project. If anything here conflicts
with the spirit of the original, the Covenant wins.
